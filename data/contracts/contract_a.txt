SUPPLIER SERVICES AGREEMENT — CONTRACT A

A01 — Parties and Purpose

This Supplier Services Agreement is entered into between Atlas Teknoloji A.Ş. (“Customer”) and Northstar Software Ltd. (“Supplier”). Supplier will provide software development and support services to Customer.

A02 — Initial Term

The Agreement begins on 1 January 2027 and continues for an initial term of twelve (12) months.

A03 — Contract Fees

The total fees payable by Customer for the initial twelve-month term are TRY 120,000.

A04 — Payment Terms

Customer shall pay each valid and undisputed invoice within forty-five (45) calendar days after receipt of the invoice.

A05 — Termination for Convenience

Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving the other party at least forty-five (45) days’ prior written notice.

A06 — Termination for Material Breach

Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately if the other party commits a material breach and fails to cure that breach within ten (10) days after receiving written notice requiring cure.

A07 — Limitation of Liability

Except for liabilities that cannot lawfully be limited, each party’s aggregate liability arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed TRY 100,000.

A08 — Confidentiality

Each party shall keep confidential all Confidential Information received from the other party and shall use such information solely for performing its obligations under this Agreement. Neither party may disclose the other party’s Confidential Information to a third party except as required by law or with the other party’s prior written consent.

A09 — Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Republic of Türkiye.

A10 — Renewal

This Agreement will not renew automatically. Any renewal or extension requires the express written agreement of both parties before expiration of the then-current term.

A11 — Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties regarding its subject matter and supersedes all prior discussions and representations.
